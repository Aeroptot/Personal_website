04 · Self-position

How I Think Mathematically

A short statement about how I approach mathematical work, kept within 100 words.

I am most interested in what happens when a clean mathematical model meets memory, uncertainty, or human behavior. I usually begin by identifying variables and constructing rules, but I have learned that the most informative part of modeling often appears when a prediction fails. An unexpected result can expose a hidden assumption, an omitted variable, or a boundary the model cannot cross. I therefore use simulations, counterexamples, and sensitivity tests not only to improve a model's performance, but also to understand what the model can and cannot claim.
